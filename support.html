Impulsar

Support

Impulsar is a landscape arcade shooter for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV: 180 missions, 75 bosses, a rescue story told over three campaigns, the Gauntlet, the Daily Run and Boss Rush.

Contact

Email softpj@icloud.com. Please include your device, its iOS or iPadOS version, the game's version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage or iPad Storage → Impulsar), and what you were doing when the problem happened. We answer within a few days.

Quick answers

Controls

Touch: hold anywhere on the left to steer with the virtual stick (or turn on tilt steering in Options → Controls), and use the Fire, Boost and Nova buttons on the right. Buttons can be swapped to the left hand, enlarged, or anchored in place. Game controllers and keyboards work too: left stick / WASD or arrow keys to move, A or Right Trigger / Space to fire, B or Left Bumper / Shift to boost, X / X key for Nova, and Menu / Esc to pause.

Progress and iCloud

Progress saves automatically on the device after every mission. Options → iCloud saves syncs missions, scores, credits, equipment and story records between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If two devices differ, the game asks which save to keep and makes a local backup first.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns off every ad placement. If you reinstall or change devices, open Options → Remove Ads and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Game Center

Leaderboards (Boss Rush, Daily Run, Campaign Total, Gauntlet) and achievements work when you are signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Options → Game Center opens the dashboard. Nothing is uploaded if you are signed out.

Performance

Options → Display offers High, Balanced and Low quality. Balanced thins the weather and glow; Low turns them off and simplifies the background, which helps on older iPhones. The game also drops detail by itself if it notices sustained slow frames.

Accessibility

Reduced motion removes camera shake, the launch and chapter cards, slow-motion beats and weather. Large buttons, a fixed stick and left-handed layouts are in Options → Controls. Audio settings include separate music, effects and ambience levels and a quiet mode that evens out loud effects.

Difficulty

Relaxed, Standard and Veteran can be changed at any time in Options → Difficulty; it applies to the campaign. Challenge modes always use Standard.

Known issues

  • Tilt steering needs a short "hold still" calibration when you switch to it; recalibrate from Options → Controls → Motion settings if the centre drifts.

Privacy

How the game handles your data is described in the privacy policy. The game has no account system; iCloud, Game Center and purchases go through Apple.
