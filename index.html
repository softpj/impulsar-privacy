Privacy policy

Clear information about your game data and privacy choices.

Effective and last updated: November 18, 2026

At a glance

You can play without creating an account.

Your progress is saved on your device. Supported signed builds can also sync game progress through your iCloud account.

On iPhone and iPad, Google AdMob may process device and advertising information when ads or consent messages are requested.

Optional tilt steering is processed on your device.

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores and achievement progress are shared with Apple's Game Center.

Privacy questions: softpj@icloud.com.

This policy explains how the developer of Impulsar (“we”, “us”) handles information in the game and when you contact us. It applies to the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV versions. Features and advertising availability can differ by platform and build.

1. Information saved on your device

The game stores mission progress, scores, credits, owned equipment, upgrades, story records and settings locally. We do not operate a player-account service or upload these game saves to our own server.

Device backups or operating-system services may keep copies under your Apple settings. Removing the app may leave backups or, on Mac, files in Application Support. Contact us if you need help finding and deleting local game data.

iCloud saves

Supported, signed Apple builds can use Apple's iCloud key-value storage to sync mission progress, scores, credits, equipment, upgrades and story records between devices signed into the same Apple account. The save includes a random game-specific device identifier, save timestamps, version information and revision history to detect conflicting saves. We do not receive your Apple account password or store a copy of your iCloud save on our own server.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game submits your best Boss Rush score, your Daily Run score, your best Gauntlet score, your total campaign score and achievement progress to Game Center. Other players may see your Game Center name and scores on leaderboards, depending on your Game Center settings. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple account details. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings app.

Controls, audio settings and advertising consent choices stay on each device. You can turn iCloud saves on or off in Options → iCloud saves. Turning syncing off does not delete copies already stored in iCloud. Apple manages iCloud storage and account access under its own privacy policy and settings. Native iCloud availability depends on the signed build, your account and device settings.

If saves differ after offline play, the game offers a choice instead of combining credit balances. It keeps a local backup before restoring another save. Apple TV can clear local cached files to recover storage, so an iCloud save is useful for keeping progress when local data is removed. Syncing can be delayed or unavailable without an internet connection or sufficient iCloud storage.

2. Advertising and Google services

The iPhone and iPad versions include Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. Test builds may show sample ads. Apple's App Store handles the optional Remove Ads purchase. The game checks its entitlement to turn off both ad placements. Ad requests can involve a network connection even when no ad is displayed.

Google's SDK may process an IP address, app or device identifiers, information about ads shown and interactions with them, and diagnostic or performance information. An IP address can indicate a general location. We do not request access to your device's location services for advertising.

The current game requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier feature, and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing for purposes such as delivering ads, measuring their performance and preventing fraud. We do not receive a copy of your device's advertising identifier in the game interface.

Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies and the choices available to you. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google's current Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures provide more detail.

Native Mac and Apple TV builds do not load the iOS AdMob SDK. A paid advertising provider for those platforms is not currently configured. If that changes, we will update this policy before introducing that provider.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required, the game uses Google's consent messages before requesting ads. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, you can reopen it from Options → Ad privacy options. You can review or change the choices the consent service makes available there. Ads may be unavailable when the necessary consent has not been obtained.

You can manage app privacy permissions in your device settings. You can also review Google's ad controls. Changing advertising choices does not remove data that was already processed; Google explains its retention and deletion tools in its privacy policy.

4. Controls and device sensors

Touch, keyboard and controller inputs are used to run the game. If you enable tilt steering on a supported device, motion readings are used locally to steer and calibrate the ship. The game does not send those readings to us or to the advertising SDK. It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location services.

5. Studio promotions and external links

The game may show promotions for other games from the same studio. The current promotion cards do not send a separate promotion-tracking event to our own server. If you follow a link to a store or another service, that service's privacy policy applies.

6. Support and TestFlight

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, record keeping or applicable requirements.

If you use TestFlight or choose to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may process usage, crash or feedback information under its own terms and privacy settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing, retention and international processing

We do not sell a player mailing list or upload your local game progress to an advertising service. Advertising providers may process information on servers outside your country and share it as described in their own policies. The in-game consent service provides region-specific choices where applicable.

Local game data remains until you remove it or the operating system removes it. iCloud copies are managed through your Apple account and can remain after the app is removed. Advertising information is retained according to Google's policies and your choices; we do not control a separate copy of those SDK records.

8. Children and privacy requests

Impulsar is a general-audience game and is not designed specifically for young children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information to us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, or to withdraw consent. Contact us about information you sent directly to us. We cannot retrieve local saves from your device or directly access Google's user records; we can help you find the relevant controls.

9. This policy page and changes

This is a static information page. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or contact forms to it. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as an IP address, to deliver and protect the page.

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and change the date at the top. Material changes that need a new choice will be presented in the app where required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about Impulsar, email softpj@icloud.com. Include the platform and app version when relevant.
